# Renamint for Mac

Official downloads, release notes, and feedback for [Renamint](https://renamint.com).

Renamint helps you organize photos and videos with local AI and reusable naming templates. Preview your changes, apply in batches, and undo from History.

- [Download the latest release](https://github.com/SufuTeam/Renamint/releases/latest)
- [Documentation](https://renamint.com/docs)
- [Pricing and licenses](https://renamint.com/pricing)
- [Report a problem or suggest a feature](https://github.com/SufuTeam/Renamint/issues/new/choose)

## Requirements

Apple silicon Mac · macOS 26 or later. Built-in AI model resources are downloaded separately (approximately 1.80 GB).

## Install

Download the DMG from Releases, open it, and drag Renamint into Applications. Release notes include the version and SHA-256 checksum.

## Free and Pro

Free includes 50 AI analyses per month. Manual naming remains free. Pro includes unlimited AI analyses, watched folders, and opt-in automatic changes in trusted folders, on up to 2 Macs.

Pro is a one-time license. USD 19.90 early bird through December 31, 2026 (Asia/Shanghai); USD 29.90 from January 1, 2027. External AI services you choose may charge separately.

## Feedback

Search existing issues first. Include your Renamint version, macOS version, and steps to reproduce. Review any screenshots before uploading. **Never publish your license key, API keys, private photos, or personal file paths.** App feedback opens a draft for you to review; it does not publish automatically.

For a missing activation email, use the [license portal](https://licenses.sufuapp.com/portal) with the email used at checkout. Do not post your email or activation code in a public issue.
